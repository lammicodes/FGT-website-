Akkaataa Programichi Hojjetu (How it Works)

  • Badhaasa Afeerraa (0.3 Birr): Namni 1 nama biroo afeeruun yoo namni sun Login gochuun fayyadamaa tajaajila keenyaa ta'uun mirkanaa'e 0.3 Birr per invitation argatta.
  • Ulaagaa Maallaqa Baafachuu (Withdrawal Rule): Yeroo jalqabaaf hanga maallaqni keessan 100 Birr ol kuufatutti baafachuun hin danda'amu. Sana booda maallaqa afeerraan argattan hanga barbaaddan baafachuu dandeessu.
  • Adeemsa Kaffaltii (Withdrawal Process): Button "Withdraw" cuqqaaluun toftaa kaffaltii (payment method) fi lakk. herregaa galchitu. Confirm yeroo gootan Pending ta'a, Adminiin arguun kaffaltii raawwata.
  • Bonus Level 2 (5%): Namoonni isin afeertan sunis akkasuma nama biroo afeeranii maallaqa yeroo withdraw godhan, maallaqa withdraw ta'e irraa 5% Bonus herrega keessan irratti galii ta'a.
