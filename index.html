About Us

Waayee Keenya

Furtu General Trading (FGT) isa teeknoolojiin deeggaramee tajaajiloota garaa garaa qulqullina olaanaadhaan dhiyeessuudha.

We provide high-quality educational tutoring, professional online training programs, and effective digital advertising services tailored to empower individuals and businesses toward success.

About Services

Ibsa Tajaajiloota Keenyaa

1. Tutoring Services / Tajaajila Barnootaa

Physical home-to-home tutoring or online individual tutoring sessions for various grade levels and subjects.

2. Training Services / Tajaajila Leenjii

Flexible online courses covering digital income creation, business planning, counseling, child treatment, and attitude change.

3. Advertising Services / Tajaajila Beeksisaa

Promoting your brand and business through social media content, custom logo design, and website features.
